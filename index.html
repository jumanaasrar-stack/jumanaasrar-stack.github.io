03 / How I work

Listen with care.
Lead with clarity.

01

Research methodology

I design studies that fit the question: interviews, surveys, segmentation, conjoint analysis, brand tracking, and visual ethnography. I build and lead research teams, set quality standards, and turn findings into decisions partners can act on.

Tools: Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey

02

User experience

I map how people move through a service, where they hesitate, and what builds their trust. Journey maps, personas, and usability testing help teams simplify onboarding and make content clear for people with different levels of language and digital literacy.

Tools: Figma, Miro, UserTesting

03

AI tools and responsible AI

I use AI tools to speed up research synthesis and content development, and I review AI-generated content for accuracy, clarity, and fairness before it reaches people. I ask whether users can understand, trust, and act on what an AI system tells them.

Tools: ChatGPT, Claude, Codex

04

Product management

I turn research into product priorities, requirements, and user stories, align stakeholders around the user's needs, and define the metrics that show whether a product is working for people.

Tools: Miro, Figma

06 / A little about me

Curiosity brought me here.
Inclusion keeps me moving.

I bring 18 years of research, insights, and program leadership across financial services, telecommunications, global development, and technology. I connect rigorous research with user experience, product decisions, and practical delivery.

I design and lead qualitative and quantitative research, selecting methods that fit the question and the people involved. My approach draws on human-computer interaction (HCI) to examine how people understand information, navigate services, and build trust. Language, digital literacy, and everyday context are central to my work.

At Algorizin, I integrate AI tools into research, product planning, and project coordination. I translate user needs into requirements, user stories, and roadmap and backlog priorities, working across functions to connect discovery with delivery. My focus is technology that people can understand, trust, and use with confidence.

People’s language belongs in the products and services built for them.
